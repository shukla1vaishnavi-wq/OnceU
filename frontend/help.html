Help & Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us if you're stuck

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OnceU match me with a mentor?

You're always matched with students exactly one year ahead of you — e.g. a 1st year sees 2nd year mentors. Branch doesn't need to match; you can pick any senior a year ahead, from any branch.

What are coins, and how do I earn them?

Every new account starts with 500 coins. You earn more by completing your mentor profile (bio, skills, photo) and, once a session is completed, coins move from junior to senior as a thank-you for the guidance.

Why does my email need a specific format?

Your college email (like yourname101btcse24@igdtuw.ac.in) is how we verify you're an IGDTUW student and auto-detect your year of study — so you don't have to set it manually, and it can't be edited later either.

Can I change my year of study manually?

No — your year is derived from your email's batch code and kept read-only in Settings, so it always stays accurate and can't be misrepresented.

What happens after I send a mentor a request?

The mentor sees your request and can accept it. Once accepted, you'll be able to chat and agree on a time slot. This full request-to-chat flow is coming in an upcoming update.

Is my profile visible to everyone?

You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security. You can toggle profile visibility, whether your college is shown, and whether seniors can send you session invitations.
