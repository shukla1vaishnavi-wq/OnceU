## 📌 Problem Statement

> Lack of structured peer mentorship and accessible experience-sharing among college students.

Every year, students face the same question: *"What should I do now?"*

| Year | The Question |
|------|--------------|
| 1st Year | Which skills should I start with? Where do I even begin? |
| 2nd Year | What should I learn next? How do I balance college, coding & projects? |
| 3rd Year | How do I prepare for internships? Which companies should I target? |
| 4th Year | How do I make the right career choices and prepare for placements? |

The answers already exist — in the **experiences of seniors**. But there's no structured way to access that knowledge. Guidance today is scattered across random DMs, WhatsApp groups, friend circles, and one-off conversations — easy to miss, hard to find, and difficult to rely on.

**The real problem isn't lack of knowledge. It's the lack of a structured bridge between experience and the students who need it.**

---

## 💡 Solution

OnceU turns scattered college knowledge into a **structured peer-mentorship network**.

Instead of searching through endless groups, random DMs, or asking around for the "right senior":

```
ASK → CONNECT → LEARN → PASS IT ON
```

- **Ask someone who's already been there** — from choosing coding languages and resources to preparing for internships and placements.
- **Get matched with the right experience** — connecting students with peers just ahead of them in their journey.
- **Make guidance a two-way exchange** — today's mentee becomes tomorrow's mentor.
- **Reward the effort, not just the outcome** — seniors earn coins for meaningful guidance, creating a system where helping others has value.

OnceU doesn't just connect students. **It creates a cycle where experience moves forward.**

### Core Highlights
- 🤝 Peer to Peer Support
- 💙 Trusted Connections
- 💰 Earn Coins for Guidance
- 📈 Grow Together, Succeed Together

## 🔄 Flow of Solution

*A simple exchange: **Experience ↔ Guidance ↔ Coins***

1. **Start** — Verify your college email, get 500 starting coins
2. **Find Your Match** — Explore mentors by branch, topic & rating
3. **Connect** — Send a request → Mentor accepts
4. **Choose Your Way to Learn** — Chat, Voice, or Video (cost scales by year & mode)
5. **Session** — Coins move from Junior → Senior, mentorship happens
6. **Close the Loop** — Junior gives a rating, senior's reputation grows, students earn coins through meaningful participation

## ⭐ Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

**Not just mentorship. A system that makes mentorship sustainable.**

1. **Guidance Has Value** — Our coin-based economy turns senior guidance from unpaid effort into a meaningful exchange.
2. **Everyone Moves Through the Loop** — Today's mentee becomes tomorrow's mentor, creating a self-sustaining cycle of knowledge.
3. **Experience-Based Matching** — Students connect with peers just ahead in their journey, making advice more relevant and relatable.
4. **Consent Before Connection** — Every interaction begins with a request → accept system. No forced conversations.
5. **Reputation Through Real Interactions** — Ratings are built after actual mentorship sessions, helping students identify reliable mentors.

**The Core Innovation:** We're not building another platform to *find* information. We're building a system to *transfer* experience.

## 🧩 Feasibility & Our Approach

| Challenge | Our Approach |
|-----------|--------------|
| Cold Start | Start with a closed pilot at IGDTUW — build a trusted community before expanding |
| Getting Seniors to Respond | Coins + ratings + visibility give mentors a reason to stay active |
| Safety & Trust | College verification + mutual consent + time limits + Report/Block |
| Technical Complexity | Use ready-made services for authentication and real-time communication, rather than building from scratch |

**Validation Loop:** IGDTUW Pilot → Real Students → Real Mentorship → Real Feedback
